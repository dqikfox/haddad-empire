Family Calendar

Give the family year a shared rhythm.

The calendar page organizes birthdays, reunions, travel, and reminders into a simple yearly structure. It works as a polished overview now and a clear handoff point for more advanced scheduling later.

Calendar purpose

  • Track key dates the family wants to protect.
  • Support reunion planning and holiday coordination.
  • Create a dependable annual cadence for shared events.
Season Focus Example entries
Q1 New-year planning Birthdays, school milestones, annual family goals.
Q2 Travel and gatherings Visits, holiday weekends, graduations, engagement events.
Q3 Reunion season Major get-togethers, group photos, memory capture, shared meals.
Q4 Reflection and celebrations Anniversaries, year-end traditions, planning for the next chapter.

Recurring events to track

  • Birthdays and anniversaries.
  • Annual reunions and recurring dinners.
  • Important travel windows and school dates.

Why this matters

Families stay connected more easily when important dates are centralized. This page gives that coordination a real destination instead of leaving it implied on the homepage.
