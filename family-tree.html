Family Tree

See the lineage, households, and generations in one place.

The family tree page is the structural heart of the portal. It maps origins, shows how households connect, and creates a clear framework for future photos, biographies, and milestone records.

Tree structure

  • Founding generation and branch roots.
  • Households grouped by lineage and marriage ties.
  • Space for birthdays, profiles, and relationship notes.

How the page organizes the family

Instead of a single dense diagram, the structure is broken into readable layers for current and future expansion.

I

Founders

The top layer records the earliest known household and sets the anchor for every branch beneath it.

II

Branches

Each major family line can hold spouses, children, and branch-specific stories without losing the wider context.

III

Profiles

Every individual can grow into a profile record with life notes, photos, achievements, and key dates.
