Documents Vault

A dedicated page for the records the family cannot afford to lose.

The vault page gives the portal a clear space for important records and sets the expectations around privacy, stewardship, and long-term preservation without pretending those private files are publicly exposed.

Archive focus

  • Certificates and identity records.
  • Estate, legal, and household documents.
  • Scanned letters, agreements, and heirloom paperwork.
A1

Identity records

Birth certificates, passports, marriage documents, and supporting records grouped by household.

A2

Estate documents

Wills, trusts, title references, and instructions that require continuity across generations.

A3

Heritage papers

Letters, migration records, translated notes, and family documents of historical value.

Category What belongs here Handling principle
Core records Certificates, IDs, and official family paperwork. Keep current, clearly named, and restricted to trusted members.
Legacy records Historical scans, elder notes, and old correspondence. Preserve with context so future generations understand significance.
Operational records Shared family admin material and important reference files. Review regularly and archive superseded versions deliberately.
